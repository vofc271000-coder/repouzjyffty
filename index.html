About Voltcrate

Voltcrate is a front-end demo of a digital gaming marketplace: currencies, keys, subscriptions, gift cards and software. Every product, price and rating here is sample data for design and testing.

FAQ

Are these products real?

No. This is a demo. Nothing here can be redeemed.

Is my cart saved?

Yes, in your browser's localStorage. It is not secure storage and not synced anywhere.

Can I pay?

No. Checkout is a demo and collects no payment details. A real store needs a payment provider and a secure backend.

Which discount codes work?

Demo codes: VOLT10 (10% off), PLAY15 (15% off), WELCOME5 (5% off).

Terms of Service

This demo site offers no real goods or services. Sample content is illustrative only. Any real deployment must replace these terms with ones reviewed by a qualified professional.

Privacy Policy

This demo stores your cart, discount code and the details you type only in your own browser (localStorage) and sends nothing to a server. Clear your browser data to remove it.
